Demon Slayer season 2 was awesome. The visuals are as always mind blowing. The fight scenes are intense, epic and extremely hype. Tengen as the Hashira is great, he is funny, super hype, I love his fighting style, his weapon and his energy. He feels different from Rengoku whilst also being similar in energy and personality. I think Daki was an interesting villain that could have had mroe development. The fight scenes toward the end were amazing. I do think that too much of season 2 is fighting the demon, like its super hype and I love the action, but I wish there was a little more character growth going on but it is still awesome. The final fight is a 10/10, its so awesome and is something I can re-watch over and over. I do think Gyutaro's reveal at the end was a bit of a let-down, I had come to be curious about Daki and was interested what made her so powerful, and then it felt like "oh actually she is not the one that is as powerful". Otherwise Gyutaro was great. I think his story is good too, simple, but makes sense and it leads to a good demon I think. Overall, though hard carried by the amazing visuals, awesome music, and greatest animated fights I have ever seen, it is a fantastic and extremely entertaining show. Not a master of storytelling and character craft, but super fun nonetheless.
